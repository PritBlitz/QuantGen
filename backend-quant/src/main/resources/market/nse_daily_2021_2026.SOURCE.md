**What was taken:** daily OHLCV from 2021-01-01 (the Lab's earliest backtest date) to 2026-01-22 (the last day in
the source), for the 14 real companies QuantGen lists. `QGEN` (QuantGen Holdings) is fictional and stays
simulated. Dates are IST trading days. Prices are split- and bonus-adjusted and rounded to 2 dp.

**Checks done:** 1,255 rows per stock with no duplicate dates; every row has low ≤ open, close ≤ high and low > 0;
the only move over 25% since 2021 is the TMPV demerger. Spot checks match known history (INFY 1,260.45 on
2021-01-01; the 2024-06-04 election-day fall; HDFC Bank's 2021 price adjusted for the 2025 1:1 bonus).

demo and portfolio project; for anything commercial, get a licensed feed.
